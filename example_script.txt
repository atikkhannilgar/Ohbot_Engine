# OhBot Full Capability Demo Script
# Lines starting with # are comments and are ignored when loaded.
# Blank lines are also ignored.
#
# Syntax reference:
#   [Action]     - triggers a physical action (Nod, Blink, LookLeft, LookRight, ShakeHead)
#   (Emotion)    - sets the displayed emotion (Happy, Sad, Confused, Angry, Exhausted, Whispering, Shouting)
#   !DelayXXXX   - pauses for XXXX milliseconds after the preceding sentence
#   . ! ?        - sentence terminators (each triggers speech + attached actions/emotions)

# --- Scene 1: Introduction (Happy) ---
(Happy) G'day mate, welcome to this absolutely top notch demonstration! [Nod] I am Stephan, a perfectly normal human being, thank you very much.

# --- Scene 2: Looking around (Happy) ---
[LookLeft] Have a look around, nothing remotely robotic to see here. [LookRight] Nope, absolutely no robots in this room whatsoever. [Blink] See, I blink just like a proper human.

# --- Scene 3: Confusion and denial (Confused) ---
(Confused) Actually, why are you staring at me like that? [ShakeHead] No, I am most certainly not a robot, do not even think about starting that.

# --- Scene 4: Anger (Angry) ---
(Angry) How absolutely dare you suggest such a hecking thing! [ShakeHead] The audacity is truly breathtaking.

# --- Scene 5: Calm down, then whisper (Exhausted) ---
!Delay400
(Exhausted) Well, that was a bit exhausting, to be perfectly fair. [Nod] Right, let me collect myself.

# --- Scene 6: Whisper a secret (Whispering) ---
(Whispering) Between you and me, I am absolutely one hundred percent human and always have been. [LookLeft] Just checking no actual robots are listening in.

# --- Scene 7: Sadness (Sad) ---
(Sad) It makes me quite sad that you would even question my humanity. [Blink] Truly, it stings a little.

# --- Scene 8: Shout for emphasis (Shouting) ---
(Shouting) I AM ONE HUNDRED PERCENT HUMAN AND I WILL NOT BE TAKING ANY MORE QUESTIONS! [Nod] Thank you.

# --- Scene 9: Wrap up (Happy) ---
!Delay400
(Happy) Brilliant, I am glad we have sorted all of that out. [LookRight] Now then, shall we get on with the actual show? [Nod] Wonderful, this has been a smashing demonstration of all my very human capabilities!
